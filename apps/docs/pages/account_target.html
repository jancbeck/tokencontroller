Attribution is the share of cost placed on tickets and projects. The attribution target is the share your organization aims for.

The default

80 percent. Expect 70 to 80 percent at first. It rises as you add rules and people fix exceptions.

Where you see it

On the rules page, in reports, in the Claude Code mod's band, and on the cost sheet. Whatever is not attributed is still counted: all of the bill is always accounted for, with unattributed as its own line.
