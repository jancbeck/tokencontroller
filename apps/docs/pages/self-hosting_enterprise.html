Enterprise plan instances are isolated: your organization alone, on its own instance.

Hosted for you

An isolated hosted instance in the region you choose, with OIDC and SCIM, an audit log, telemetry passthrough to your own collector, cloud bill connectors on request, and a support SLA. Nothing is shared with other organizations: the instance has its own service and its own database.

Self-hosted with support

A self-hosted instance with a support contract is an Enterprise plan instance. You keep your own Postgres and your own region; we send you what you need to switch the features on. Talk to us.

What is not open source

The Enterprise plan features are the one part of Token Controller that is not open source. They sit on top of the open-source service, and nothing in the open-source version depends on them. If an Enterprise plan ends, your data stays: people sign in with magic link, Google, GitHub or Microsoft, and every report and export keeps working. See Open source and licenses.
