An organization is one customer of Token Controller: its people, teams, projects and periods. Its people sit in the team tree.

Organization settings

SettingWhat it does
Cost sourceHow your provider charges you. It decides what the close reconciles against; see Bills.
Attribution targetThe share of cost you aim to place on tickets and projects. Default 80 percent.
ApprovalOn or off. See Approval.
Sign-inWhich sign-in providers members may use, and which email domains.

The team tree

People are arranged as person, team, department, organization, following your org chart. A department is usually a discipline such as engineering, design or QA. Each person belongs to one team at a time. Every node is a cost center, so a team's cost is the sum of its people's.

The team tree is separate from projects on purpose: one person can work on several projects but belongs to one team.

When someone moves

When a person changes team or leaves, only future token entries follow. Past postings keep the team they had, so last month's reports do not change.
